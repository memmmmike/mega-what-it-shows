The queued parser fix closes only the first row. Day-first tenants who type through the booking-v3 hint, and the reports where a date moved by one day, stay broken after it ships.

How the reports were counted

  • Once per report. The ticket export comes in overlapping pages, so a report can appear twice. Counted once by id.
  • Cause by tenant, not by tag. Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser. The cause comes from the tenant: the locale they write dates in and the booking form they run. The “intake tagged” column shows how often the two disagree.
  • A swap is not a shift. Swapping day and month cannot move a date by one day. The reports the desk read as a one-day move are a different bug, so they leave the parser count.

Why one ticket hid three

#4471 was built by decision 0001: merge on the subject line, without reading the body. A subject says what the reporter saw, a wrong date, not what broke. Different bugs with the same symptom land in one ticket, and the count then reads as one bug reported many times.

Before merging a date report into an open ticket, check its tenant’s locale and booking form, and whether the date swapped or shifted.
